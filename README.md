# Why Is It Called That? 🤔

**Explaining the origins and meanings of common software engineering terms**

## 🎯 Purpose

This resource helps non-native English speakers understand the **etymology** and **meaning** behind common software engineering terms. By understanding where these words come from, you'll remember their purpose forever without needing to memorize definitions!

## 🌟 Why This Matters

Many programming terms have interesting origins that, once understood, make their usage intuitive:
- **Bug** comes from an actual moth found in early computers
- **Cookie** is named after fortune cookies that contain hidden messages
- **Daemon** comes from Maxwell's demon, a helpful background entity
- **Cache** comes from French "cacher" meaning "to hide"

Understanding these origins helps you:
- ✅ Remember terms naturally instead of memorizing
- ✅ Use terms correctly in context
- ✅ Feel more confident in technical discussions
- ✅ Build vocabulary organically
